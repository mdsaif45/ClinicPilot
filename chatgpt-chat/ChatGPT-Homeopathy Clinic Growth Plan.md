This is the **most important question** before building the app.

## If you build it properly, include these safeguards

### 1. User authentication
- Strong password or passphrase.
- Optional fingerprint/Face ID login.
- Two-factor authentication (recommended).

### 2. Encryption
- Encrypt data while it's stored on the server.
- Encrypt data when it's sent between the app and the server (HTTPS/TLS).

If someone intercepted the traffic, they shouldn't be able to read the records.

### 3. Automatic cloud backup
If your phone is lost or damaged, your patient data shouldn't disappear.

### 4. Role-based access
If you later have a receptionist:
- Receptionist: appointments, billing.
- Doctor: full medical records.
- Assistant: only the permissions you choose.

### 5. Audit log
Record:
- Who viewed a record.
- Who edited it.
- When it was changed.

This helps with accountability.

### 6. Consent management
For patient photos or case sharing:
- Record whether the patient gave consent.
- Never publish identifiable information without permission.

### 7. Secure sessions
Automatically log out after a period of inactivity, especially on shared devices.
